# Crossroads world art shopping list

Start with one downtown intersection: an office lobby, an apartment entrance, a grocery, a service alley and a wrecked van. The player should recognize each entrance from across the street and still see convincing door hardware, window frames and shop fittings from arm's length. Approve that block in the game before collecting a whole city of similar assets.

## What to push

Put the original archive in `World/<kit-name>/` on the **content** branch with a small text file naming the author, source URL, license and any credited third-party textures. Prefer GLB with embedded maps, or FBX/Blend with the matching texture folder. A USDZ with sound UVs, material assignments and original maps is also useful. For a restricted marketplace asset, share the listing and dependency manifest and keep the raw files in storage allowed by its license.

- Target 1K maps for ordinary props and repeated small pieces. Target 2K PBR sets or trim sheets for architecture, prominent equipment, doors, interiors and vehicles. A 2K tile covering 4 m gives 512 pixels/m; stretched across a 40 m façade it gives 51 pixels/m. Tiling and trim sheets matter more than the label on the archive.
- For the first sample, include a door, corner and complete wall bay with base color, normal and roughness. Include metallic or packed ORM where appropriate. Keep glass on a separate material so opaque walls and pumps render correctly.
- Retain real units, original pivots and door leaves. Include open doorways, interior wall backs and sensible collision or separable geometry for walkable buildings. We attach the game's interactive, destructible doors and loot actors during integration.
- For a scan, include an eye-level view of the base, doorway and side wall. Ground skirts, holes behind windows, baked shadows and a mushy underside show up immediately in first person. A good skyline scan can still be used at a distance.
- Repeated props must share meshes and materials. Profile the downtown block on target hardware with streaming, collisions, active loot and enemies before raising geometry or texture budgets for the rest of the map.

The suburban shops use the counter, register and refrigerated case from Dawid’s Post-Soviet Shop Asset Pack. Each prop was separated, centered and exported with reduced color, normal, roughness and metallic maps.
